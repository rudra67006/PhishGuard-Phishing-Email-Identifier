Phishing Detection Checklist

Check for these warning signs whenever you receive an email.

01. Unknown or suspicious sender

Check the complete sender address. Attackers may use addresses that imitate trusted organizations.

02. Urgent or threatening language

Messages saying “Act now” or “Your account will be blocked” may pressure you into making mistakes.

03. Suspicious or mismatched links

Check where a link actually leads. A displayed company name may not match the destination website.

04. Requests for sensitive information

Be suspicious of unexpected requests for passwords, OTPs, banking details, or login credentials.

05. Unexpected attachments

Unexpected files may contain malware. Verify the message with the sender before opening attachments.

06. Suspicious spelling or unusual offers

Unexpected prizes, strange wording, or offers that seem too good to be true deserve extra scrutiny.

Sample Email Analysis

These fictional examples demonstrate common phishing signs. The example links use reserved invalid domains and are not intended to be opened.

Sample 1: Urgent Account Warning

From: account-alert@example.invalid

Subject: Final Warning — Account Will Be Blocked

Your account will be blocked immediately. Verify your password within 10 minutes to avoid losing access. Visit https://account-check.example.invalid to continue.

Show suspicious signs
  • Uses threatening and urgent language.
  • Creates pressure with a 10-minute deadline.
  • Requests a password.
  • Uses an unfamiliar example domain.

Sample 2: Suspicious Bank Message

From: security-team@example.invalid

Subject: Verify Your Banking Details

We detected a problem with your account. Confirm your banking details and OTP at https://unknown-bank.example.invalid to prevent account suspension.

Show suspicious signs
  • Requests sensitive banking information and an OTP.
  • Uses fear of account suspension.
  • The sender identity is not verified.
  • The destination domain does not establish that it belongs to a real bank.

Sample 3: Prize Notification

From: winner-notice@example.invalid

Subject: Congratulations! Claim Your Prize Now

You have won a special reward! Claim it immediately by visiting https://claim-prize.example.invalid. Provide your personal details to receive your prize.

Show suspicious signs
  • Unexpected prize or reward.
  • Urgent instruction to claim immediately.
  • Requests personal information.
  • Unknown sender and unverified destination.

Sample 4: Unexpected Attachment

From: document-sharing@example.invalid

Subject: Invoice Overdue — Open Immediately

Your invoice is overdue. Open the attached file named Invoice_Update.zip immediately to avoid a penalty. Contact us only through the details in this email.

Show suspicious signs
  • Pressures the recipient to act immediately.
  • Includes an unexpected compressed attachment.
  • Uses a possible financial penalty to create fear.
  • Sender identity and invoice should be verified independently.

Short Findings Report

Project: Phishing Email Identifier

Objective: Identify common warning signs in suspicious emails.

Method

Four fictional email examples were reviewed using a checklist covering sender identity, urgent language, suspicious links, sensitive-information requests, and unexpected attachments.

Findings

  • All four examples contain multiple warning signs.
  • Urgency and fear are used to pressure recipients.
  • Some messages request sensitive personal or financial information.
  • Unknown senders, unverified links, and unexpected attachments require caution.

Conclusion

Users should verify the sender through an independent trusted channel, inspect link destinations carefully, avoid sharing passwords or OTPs, and never open unexpected attachments without verification.

Important: This is an educational demonstration. Real phishing emails may look professional, and the absence of these warning signs does not prove that an email is safe.
