What should you check?

  • Does the message pressure you to act immediately?
  • Is the sender's email address unfamiliar or unexpected?
  • Does a link lead somewhere different from what you expect?
  • Does the email ask for a password, OTP, or banking details?
  • Were you expecting any attachments in the message?
